Available 24/7 Web & Ground Floor Kiosks

Online Public Access Catalogue (OPAC)

Search our comprehensive collection of more than 45,000 print volumes, journals, maps, and special collections directly from any web browser or dedicated on-site touch kiosk.

  • Instant search by book title, author name, subject keyword, or ISBN.
  • Real-time shelf status indicator showing exact shelf location and copy availability.
  • One-click hold placement with automated SMS and email notifications when ready.
  • Remote loan renewal feature to extend borrowing periods without visiting in person.

Availability & Access Details

Online Portal Hours:
Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
On-Site Touch Kiosks:
Mon – Fri: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM | Sat: 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
Eligibility:
Search is open to everyone; holds require an active Member ID
Service Fee:
Completely Free of charge

Steps to Access the Online Catalogue:

  1. Visit the OPAC web address or tap any search kiosk on the Ground Floor.
  2. Enter your search query in the search bar and filter by category or shelf location.
  3. Log in with your 10-digit Library Card Number and 4-digit PIN to place a hold.
  4. Receive your pickup confirmation alert and collect the title from the hold shelf within 5 days.

Available 24/7 Worldwide Mobile App & Web Reader

Haven Cloud E-Books & Audiobooks

Borrow bestselling novels, academic textbooks, scholarly papers, and professional audiobooks anywhere in the world on your smartphone, tablet, Kindle, or desktop computer.

  • Over 12,000 digital titles available in EPUB, PDF, and high-quality MP3 audio formats.
  • Offline reading mode enabled — download titles once and read without an active internet connection.
  • Zero late return fees: borrowed e-books are automatically checked back in at the end of the loan period.
  • Cross-device synchronization for bookmarks, highlights, reading progress, and margin notes.

Availability & Access Details

Platform Availability:
24/7 uninterrupted worldwide cloud access
Borrowing Limits:
Up to 5 e-books and 3 audiobooks checked out simultaneously
Lending Period:
21 days (with one optional 7-day renewal if no waitlist)
Compatible Devices:
iOS, Android, Kindle Paperwhite, Windows, macOS, ChromeOS

Steps to Borrow E-Books & Audiobooks:

  1. Download the Haven Cloud Reader app from Google Play or the App Store, or visit the web reader.
  2. Search for and select "Readers' Haven Public Library - Lahore" as your local library.
  3. Enter your active Library Membership Number and password to link your library account.
  4. Browse available titles, tap "Borrow", and start reading or listening instantly.

Quiet Study Pods & Collaborative Media Rooms

Four individual sound-insulated study pods and two multimedia group collaboration suites designed for deep uninterrupted study, video meetings, and group academic research.

  • Individual Quiet Pods (Rooms 201–204): 1–2 person capacity with ergonomic seating and task lighting.
  • Group Media Suites (Rooms 205–206): 3–6 person capacity with 55" 4K wall displays and HDMI inputs.
  • Full acoustic sound-dampening insulation to ensure total concentration and confidentiality.
  • Wall-mounted dry-erase glass whiteboards with marker sets and erasers supplied at check-in.

Availability & Access Details

Monday to Friday:
9:15 AM to 6:45 PM (Last slot starts at 5:00 PM)
Saturday:
10:15 AM to 3:45 PM (Last slot starts at 2:00 PM)
Booking Duration:
2-hour sessions (Extendable by 1 hour if no upcoming reservation)
Booking Window:
Book up to 7 days in advance online or at the Level 2 Desk

Steps to Reserve a Study Room:

  1. Log in to your library account online or consult the Digital Services desk on Level 2.
  2. Select your preferred room type (Individual Quiet Pod or Group Media Suite) and preferred time slot.
  3. Confirm your booking to receive an instant reservation confirmation code via email and SMS.
  4. Arrive 5 minutes before your slot and present your membership card to collect your smart access keycard.

High-Speed Free Wi-Fi & Computer Lab

Stay connected across every corner of the library with our high-speed symmetrical fiber Wi-Fi, or log on to 24 high-performance multimedia workstations in our modern First Floor Computer Lab.

  • 100 Mbps symmetric fiber connection with enterprise WPA2 security encryption.
  • 24 desktop workstations pre-installed with Microsoft Office, Python, VS Code, SPSS, and GIMP.
  • Dedicated accessibility stations equipped with NVDA screen readers and large-type keyboards.
  • Courtyard outdoor Wi-Fi coverage extending past reading halls until 9:00 PM daily.

Steps to Connect to Wi-Fi and Use Public PCs:

  2. Open any web browser; the library welcome portal will appear automatically.
  3. Enter your membership number, or request a complimentary visitor guest code at the reception.
  4. Accept the library's acceptable use policy to activate high-speed internet access.

Wireless Printing, Document Scanning & Copying

Send print jobs directly from your laptop, smartphone, or home computer before visiting, or use our high-resolution duplex color laser scanners and photocopiers in the Ground Floor IT Hub.

  • Mobile Cloud Printing: forward documents via email or web upload without installing drivers.
  • Secure PIN release: your print jobs are held safely in the queue until you enter your personal PIN.
  • High-resolution 600 DPI optical scanning with automated OCR text recognition to USB or email.
  • Affordable subsidized rates for students and researchers with digital wallet payment support.

Availability & Access Details

Print Hub Hours:
Mon – Fri: 9:00 AM – 6:30 PM | Sat: 10:00 AM – 3:30 PM
Print Rates (B&W):
PKR 5 per A4 single page (PKR 8 per double-sided sheet)
Print Rates (Color):
PKR 20 per A4 page
Scanning & OCR:
100% Free (Scan directly to email or personal USB drive)

Steps to Print or Scan Documents:

  1. Email your documents as attachments to print@readershaven.org or upload via the web terminal.
  2. Check your email inbox or screen for a unique 6-digit release PIN code.
  3. Go to any multifunction printer station located in the Ground Floor IT Hub.
  4. Type your 6-digit PIN on the touchscreen, review your preview, and press "Release Print".
