How to become a member

  1. Choose the membership plan that suits your needs.
  2. Visit Readers' Haven Library with a valid ID. Students should bring their student ID.
  3. Complete the membership registration and pay the annual fee.
  4. Collect your library card and start borrowing books.

Borrowing reminders

Return borrowed books within your plan's loan period. Late fees apply to each overdue book for every additional day.

Need help selecting a plan? Our library team is happy to guide you.
