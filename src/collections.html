How to find & borrow books

  1. Note the Call Number (such as FIC • HOS) shown on each title card.
  2. Visit the designated floor wing or check real-time shelf availability on our Ground Floor OPAC kiosks.
  3. Present your active library card at the Circulation Desk to borrow Fiction, Non-Fiction, Urdu, or Children's books.
Compare membership borrowing limits →

Reading room & loan guidelines

  • Circulating Collections: Fiction, Non-Fiction, Urdu Literature, and Children's titles can be taken home for 14 to 28 days depending on your plan.
  • Reference Volumes: Dictionaries, atlases, and encyclopedias remain in the Level 2 Reading Room so every visitor can consult them.
  • Free Scanning: Need pages from a Reference book? Use the free OCR scanners in the Ground Floor IT Hub.
